# Demystifying the Submission and Peer Review Process (2024)

## Instructor

Melissa Cantrell\
Scholarly Communication Librarian\
Center for Research Data & Digital Scholarship

## Workshop Description

Why is the scholarly publishing and peer review process the way it is, and what exactly happens between submission and acceptance of a publication? This workshop will deconstruct submission and peer review by outlining disciplinary practices and discussing what to expect from different kinds of review. After this workshop you’ll have the knowledge you need to start hacking submission and peer review.
