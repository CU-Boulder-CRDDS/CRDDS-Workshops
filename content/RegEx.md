# Using Regular Expressions in Digital Research! (2019)

## Instructor

Kevan A. Feshami\
Graduate Assistant\
Center for Research Data & Digital Scholarship\
University of Colorado Boulder Libraries

## Workshop Description

This workshop explores the use of regular expressions to find different variations on the same terms in digital corpora. The value of using regular expressions will be covered. Learners will also work through the rules of writing regular expressions through a hands-on exercise.
