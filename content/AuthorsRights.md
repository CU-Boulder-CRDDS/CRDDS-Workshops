# Authors’ Rights in a Rapidly Changing Scholarly Ecosystem (2025)

## Instructor

Melissa Cantrell\
Scholarly Communication Librarian\
Center for Research Data & Digital Scholarship

## Workshop Description

What rights do you have over your own publications? How does open access enable you to share and disseminate your work? How does the use of artificial intelligence (AI) impact ownership? We’ll explore all this and more, starting with the basics of authors’ rights and copyright and delving into how current trends in publishing will impact scholarship of the future.
