# Navigating Open Access Publishing Options at CU Boulder (2026)

## Instructor

Melissa Cantrell\
Scholarly Communication Librarian\
Center for Research Data & Digital Scholarship

## Workshop Description

What are the benefits of sharing my work openly? How can I retain my rights as an author? What are the best strategies for increasing visibility of my research? In this workshop we’ll delve into these questions and more, as well as provide tips for how to find the best publishing resources provided through CU Boulder. This workshop is for students and faculty getting started with scholarly publishing.
